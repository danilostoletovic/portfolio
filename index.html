BEFORE WE BUILD

A few practical questions.

No pitch deck needed. Start with what you know.

Tell me about your project ↓
Is my project too small?

A focused fix, a landing page, or a prototype can be a sensible starting point. Send me the problem and what you already have; we can work out whether a small, clearly scoped engagement fits.

How do we discuss price?

Price depends on scope, integrations, existing code, and the support you need. Share your goal, any budget range, and constraints. We agree on the work and cost before development starts.

How soon could it be ready?

Timing depends on the scope, my availability, and what needs to be supplied or integrated. Tell me about your deadline early. We discuss a realistic plan before agreeing to the project; there is no one-size-fits-all turnaround.

What happens after I contact you?

We clarify the problem, users, and constraints. I suggest an approach and we agree on scope, timing, and cost. You review working software as the build progresses, then receive the agreed delivery and handover.

What about maintenance after launch?

Bug fixes, updates, hosting help, and ongoing maintenance can be discussed as part of the scope. We agree on what the handover includes and which support, if any, continues after launch.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. I work remotely with international clients. We agree on communication, review points, and any time-zone constraints at the start. You work directly with me.

EDGE AI / ROBOTICS

SmartVehicle

  • Raspberry Pi 5
  • Python / YOLO
  • Kotlin / Compose
My role
Raspberry Pi services and native Android control client
Useful for your project
Connected systems and Android apps

The short version.

Vision, movement, and networking — working together on a vehicle with actual wheels.

Why I built it

A busy vision pipeline should not make the operator wait to control the vehicle.

What I built

I built a Raspberry Pi 5 vision platform and a native Android control station, connecting live video, motor commands, and telemetry.

The important engineering decision

Camera capture, inference and movement have different jobs. Multithreaded pipelines and separate Docker services keep expensive vision work from owning the control path. Smart Network supplies a local fallback hotspot when external Wi-Fi drops.

Actual work. Open for inspection.

What came out of the build

4th place and a Special Award at the 10th Galaksija Cup. The prototype, Android interface, and source are available to inspect.

Why I built it

Visitors should be able to ask about a project or explain their idea without knowing which technology or service to search for.

TRY A REAL CONVERSATION

What I built

I built the lightweight chat interface and integrated a separate Cloudflare Secretary Worker. Ana helps visitors explore public project, service, and professional information.

The important engineering decision

The portfolio stays useful without the assistant. The Worker validates requests, applies rate admission and exact CORS restrictions, and keeps credentials on the server. Conversations use visitor-supplied history, without persisted server tasks.

The short version.

Rotating weeks. Local reminders. A school day that makes sense.

Why I built it

A changing timetable needs to answer one simple question quickly: what comes next?

What I built

A Flutter application with date-aware one-to-eight-week rotations, subject colors, local reminders, and explicit JSON backup and restore.

The important engineering decision

Share the scheduling logic, but handle notifications, file saving and release packaging as platform concerns. Backup and restore are explicit transfers, not automatic cloud sync.

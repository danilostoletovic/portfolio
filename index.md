## Things I've built

### YOLO Smart Vehicle

A Raspberry Pi 5 vehicle that sees the world with YOLO and takes instructions from a custom Android app. Computer vision, motor control, and live video. Camera capture, inference, and motor control are kept separate so a busy vision pipeline doesn't hold up the vehicle.

- Technologies: Python, Raspberry Pi 5, Kotlin / Jetpack Compose, Docker.
- 4th place + Special Award at the 10th Galaksija Cup National STEM Championship.
- 30 FPS camera feed, on-device detection, live video and telemetry.

Start with what the site needs to do

You may need a first website, a redesign, or a landing page for a specific offer. We start with the audience and the useful next action: an enquiry, booking, application, or purchase through an agreed external service.

A small business site often needs clear content, dependable forms, and straightforward maintenance. The implementation should fit that job and the people who will maintain it.

What we can include

  • Responsive implementation for phones, tablets, and desktop.
  • Clear navigation, readable content, and accessible interaction patterns.
  • Performance-conscious images and lightweight page delivery.
  • SEO foundations: page titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, internal links, and sitemap.
  • Contact forms, booking services, API connections, and automation.
  • Domain, DNS, Cloudflare, hosting, and business email configuration.
  • Deployment, source handover, and agreed maintenance guidance.

Content, integrations, hosting costs, and ongoing support are agreed as part of the scope. Search visibility depends on competition, useful content, links, and indexing; a website build does not guarantee a ranking.

How we get it online

  1. Define the audience, offer, and main contact or conversion action.
  2. Review existing content, brand materials, and domain setup.
  3. Agree on pages, integrations, implementation, and delivery scope.
  4. Build and check the site across screen sizes.
  5. Deploy and hand over the agreed setup.

Questions before starting

Can you help with my domain and hosting?

Yes. DNS, Cloudflare, hosting, and email setup can be included in the agreed scope. I will first review the current setup and required services.

Can the website have an AI assistant?

Yes, where it solves a real visitor problem. We need to define its approved information, expected questions, operating costs, and human handoff.

Will the site rank first on Google?

No ranking can be promised. I can implement the technical foundations and useful page structure. Search Console and real enquiry data help evaluate results after launch.

How do you price a website?

Pricing depends on content, number of pages, integrations, and support needs. Share your goal, existing site, and timing so we can discuss scope.

Send me the site and the problem

If you already have a website, include its URL and what you want improved. For a new one, tell me what your business does, who it serves, and what visitors should do next.
